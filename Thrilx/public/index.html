Questions, answered.

Where does the streaming availability come from?

TMDB's watch provider data, supplied by JustWatch. It varies by country and changes often, so check the service before you subscribe.

What is "My list"?

A watchlist saved in this browser. It isn't tied to an account, so it won't follow you to another device.

A title or detail looks wrong. What do I do?

Details come straight from TMDB, so corrections made there appear here. For anything else, use the contact section below.
